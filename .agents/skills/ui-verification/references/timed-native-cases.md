# Timed native cases

Use for native SDK/manual-barrier cases whose framework deadline includes operator
observations or input. Ordinary screenshots do not acquire a timed-case/report requirement.
Keep existing [authorship and ownership boundaries](../../delegation/SKILL.md), including
mandatory whole-run retirement before HOST work through [finalization](../../finalization/SKILL.md).

## Prepare before launch

Have the existing phase commands, privacy-safe projections, expected identities/report
contract and qualified restoration inputs ready before launching the timed case. Resolve
essential Source/tool questions and preservation prerequisites beforehand; an active case
is not preparation time. Use the owning device procedure, without a new harness or script.

Before launch, record a phase-specific action/capture plan and realistic operator time budget,
using measured comparable timings or explicit conservative estimates. Include model/tool
round trips and settlement, not only shell duration. Assign intermediate cumulative elapsed-time
checkpoints that leave time for all remaining phases and a protected final-phase reserve.
Fix that reserve and its phase-entry cutoff before launch: cover final original/OS checks,
Continue, end assertions, required report and in-case teardown. Do not reduce it mid-case after
a budget shortfall. It is allocated time consumed by that phase, not a fresh full-duration
minimum after each completed action; remaining actions still need their planned time and guards.
Budget normal restoration separately at its owning phase; never restore early while the case
requires continuity.

## Track the actual budget

Bind the effective framework limit to the actual case start and record its deadline;
a later driver attach or shell invocation does not restart that budget. At each planned
intermediate checkpoint, compare elapsed time with the remaining plan and protected final
reserve; do not wait until the final barrier to detect overrun. Reassess before progression.
During the live case, limit model work to the immediate current original/OS checks and required
input, retaining only the case's necessary observation/identity/time facts. Defer correlation prose, research
and extra bookkeeping until terminal. Honor stop/scope steering; otherwise answer briefly and
recompute the budget before progressing. Do not bypass current-image or input-authority guards.
If a necessary unknown or insufficient reserve prevents completion, stop progression and
retain the actual incomplete/interrupted outcome rather than rushing past observation guards.
Do not extend the timeout, restart the clock or substitute fixed sleeps for case settlement.

## Resolve the actual result

Read the case/framework terminal outcome as well as the wrapper/driver result. Framework
failure or timeout wins over exit0, a printed success line or stock-wrapper completion.
Keep the [state/artifact guards](state-and-artifacts.md), including current input authority,
original-image inspection and actual leaf-result handling.

Require the owning case's terminal assertions and every required stage to complete. Seeing
the final barrier label does not prove its observation/Continue or final assertions finished.
Where the contract expects structured report data, require a parsed, non-null, case-bound
completed report with the expected end assertions/stages. Missing/null/partial required reports are
not PASS, even with a successful wrapper. Preserve failures and attributable partial originals;
they support only their observed phases, not successful completion of the expired case.

## Close a failed or expired case

An expired/failed case cannot resume acceptance from its last visible barrier or memory.
Its remaining state-changing work is only qualified case-owned cleanup and normal restoration.
Follow the owning procedure's teardown order and required driver/process termination before
operations that depend on it. Preserve the failure, partial evidence and legitimate newer
state; inspect restoration originals before claiming it.
Do not replay barriers, manufacture permission eligibility, reset bookkeeping/data or erase
user-decision history to obtain another attempt. Any later case follows its existing scope
and fresh prerequisites; this reference adds no approval flow or authority to reset/retry.
