## Using WardPulse

WardPulse displays usage, allowances and spending reported by the services you connect.
Use your existing provider accounts or reporting credentials where required. Each provider
sets the terms and charges for its own subscriptions and API usage. An internet connection
is required for sign-in and fresh reports. If a refresh is delayed or fails, the app may
show the last successful report and its update time.

You can use the Android app on its own. The Wear OS app runs on Wear OS 4 or later and
receives usage summaries from a paired Android phone. The WardPulse watch face requires
Wear OS 5 or later and may need to be installed separately on compatible watches.
See the [User Guide][guide] and the applicable Google Play listings for compatibility,
availability and setup.
