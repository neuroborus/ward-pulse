# Wear OS store gallery

Sample usage data is shown throughout. The captures use the fixed dark appearance of the
Wear app and watch face. Files were added and reviewed on 2026-11-22. Glance and Plan
windows were captured in one session on the round Wear emulator on 2026-11-22. The watch
face screenshots were captured on 2026-11-06 (Europe/Paris) and show the same sample
readings with different ring selections. All four screenshots are 384 × 384 RGB PNGs.

| Repository order | File | Content |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0 | [Promotional cover](0-cover.png) | 1254 × 1254 RGB art with the watch face, Glance and phone Dashboard in illustrated, unbranded hardware. |
| 1 | [Watch face with three bands](1-watch-face-three-bands.png) | Full watch face with selected usage and available credits. |
| 2 | [Glance](2-glance.png) | Remaining capacity and purchased credits. |
| 3 | [Plan windows](3-plan-windows.png) | A reported allowance and its reset time. |
| 4 | [Watch face with two bands](4-watch-face-two-bands.png) | Two widened bands with three selected usage readings. |

Use slots 2 and 3 in the Google Play Wear OS app listing. Use slots 1 and 4 in the separate
watch face listing. Slot 0 is promotional art. Google Play requires unframed screenshots of
the app or watch face in each listing.

[Google Play preview asset requirements](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151?hl=en-GB)
